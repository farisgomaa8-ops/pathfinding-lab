Breadth-first search

Explores one step at a time. Finds a route with the fewest steps, but treats expensive terrain like ordinary ground.

Dijkstra

Explores the cheapest known route next. Finds the lowest travel cost, including the cost of crossing terrain.

A* search

Adds a distance estimate to the known cost. With this grid’s movement rules, the estimate keeps the route optimal.
